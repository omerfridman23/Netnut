# Seed data

The seed (`backend/prisma/seed.ts`) creates ~10 products and ~10 customers covering the scenarios the dashboard demonstrates:

- Heavy consumer (250 events of history)
- Near-zero balance (low-balance warning)
- Zero balance (empty-wallet indicator)
- Recently credited customer
- Customer with no consumption history (empty-state UI)
